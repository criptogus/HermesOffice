# Workshop deck template

Interactive session deck: instructions, exercises, timeboxes.

## Style

- Palette: white background, friendly indigo #4F46E5 primary, amber #F59E0B for timers/callouts.
- Typography: rounded sans feel; instruction steps numbered and large (28px+), readable from the back of a room.
- Layout: recurring slide types — section divider (color-blocked), instruction slide (numbered steps + timebox badge), discussion prompt (single big question), recap checklist.
- Callout chips: 'Exercise', '10 min', 'In pairs' rendered as rounded badges top-right.
- Every exercise slide states: goal, steps, time, and what to have ready at the end.

## Suggested page plan

1. Welcome + goals of the session
2. Agenda with timeboxes
3. Icebreaker (Exercise, 5 min)
4. Concept intro
5. Exercise 1 (steps + 15 min)
6. Group discussion prompt
7. Exercise 2 (steps + 20 min)
8. Recap checklist + next steps

## Agent notes

- Keep the page count close to the plan; merge or split pages based on the user's actual content, never to pad.
- Follow the deck-wide palette and typography exactly; consistency beats variety.
- Figures require a declared dataSource ('user' / 'document' / 'search' / 'sample'); sample data must be flagged to the user.
